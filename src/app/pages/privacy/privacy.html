PRIVACY INFORMATION

How We Handle Your Information

Learn what information the system stores and how your submitted concerns are handled.

◉

Anonymous Submission

The submission form does not ask for your full name or student ID. Avoid including personal details in your concern.

▤

Information Collected

The system stores your selected category, subject, description, submission date, tracking code, and concern status.

⌕

Tracking Code

A unique tracking code is generated after submission. Keep it safe because it is used to check your concern's progress.

▣

Data Storage

In the current demonstration version, submissions are stored in your browser's local storage. They are not yet stored in a shared central database.

✓

Responsible Use

Submit accurate, respectful, and relevant information. Do not include passwords or unnecessary personal information.

!

Important Reminder

Because this is currently a browser-based demonstration, privacy and confidentiality are not guaranteed by server-side security controls.

ⓘ

Before You Submit a Concern

Review your message and remove personal details that are not necessary to explain the issue. Keep your tracking code in a safe place.
